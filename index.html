CHEM 101 Practice Activity

Atomic Structure War

In each hand, you and the computer will receive one Atomic Structure War card. Determine the numerical value represented by each card, then choose the card with the larger value.

Completion goal

  • Complete all 28 hands.
  • Choose the winning card correctly on at least 70% of the hands.
  • A score of 20 correct out of 28 meets the 70% requirement.
  • After each choice, the card values are shown so you can check your reasoning.
  • If you meet both requirements, the game generates a completion certificate with your name.
